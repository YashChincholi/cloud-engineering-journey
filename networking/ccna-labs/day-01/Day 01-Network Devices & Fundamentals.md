# Free CCNA 200-301 Course: Day 1 - Network Devices

The primary focus of day 1 is to define what a network is and introduce fundamental network devices and their functions.

## Key Concepts and Network Devices:

*   **What is a Network?** A digital communications network that allows interconnected devices (nodes) to share resources.
*   **Clients:** Devices that access services provided by servers. Examples include smartphones, laptops, and desktop PCs used to watch this video.
*   **Servers:** Devices that provide functions or services to clients. This can range from dedicated hardware in data centers to a PC sharing a file or a smartphone sending data.
*   **Switches:**
    *   Used to connect multiple end hosts (like PCs and servers) within the **same Local Area Network (LAN)**.
    *   They have numerous network interfaces (ports) for connecting devices.
    *   Switches forward traffic **within** a LAN but **do not** provide connectivity between different LANs or over the internet.
*   **Routers:**
    *   Designed to connect **different LANs** together and provide connectivity **over the internet**.
    *   They typically have fewer network interfaces compared to switches.
    *   Routers are essential for directing traffic between separate networks.
*   **Firewalls:**
    *   Specialized **network security devices** that monitor and control network traffic entering and exiting a network.
    *   They operate based on **configured security rules** to allow or deny traffic, protecting the network from unauthorized access.
    *   Firewalls can be placed inside or outside the network, often near the router.
    *   **Network firewalls** are hardware devices, while **host-based firewalls** are software applications on individual devices (like your PC's firewall).
